On Reading Slowly

Most of what I know about patience I learned from dictionaries. A good one never hurries you. You arrive with a word you only half understand, , say, and leave ten minutes later having met its neighbours, its ancestors, and two or three you were not looking for.

The habit is work disguised as idling. Every of lookups is a small walk. Some days the walk is ; on others it away with the afternoon, and the page you meant to finish waits where you left it.
